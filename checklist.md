# Checklist for Plain Language

> Part of [Checklists](checklists.md)


Use this checklist to see if your document meets plain language standards. (If you're writing for the web, here's the [web checklist]({{ site.url }}{% link _pages/resources/checklists/web-checklist.md %}).)

- [written for the average reader](audience.md)
- [organized to serve the reader's needs](organize.md)
- [has useful headings](add-useful-headings.md)
- [uses "you" and other pronouns to speak to the reader](address-the-user.md)
- [uses active voice](use-active-voice.md)
- uses [short sections](write-short-sections.md) and [short sentences](write-short-sentences.md)
- [uses the simplest tense possible](use-the-present-tense.md)—simple present is best
- [uses base verbs, not hidden verbs](avoid-hidden-verbs.md)
- [omits excess words](concise.md)
- [uses concrete, familiar words](use-simple-words-phrases.md)
- [uses "must" to express requirements; avoids the ambiguous word "shall"](use-must-to-indicate-requirements.md)
- [places words carefully](place-words-carefully.md) (avoids large gaps between the subject, the verb and the object; puts exceptions last; places modifiers correctly)
- uses [lists](use-lists.md) and [tables](use-tables-to-make-complex-material-easier-to-understand.md) to simplify complex material
- [uses no more than two or three subordinate levels]({{ site.url }})
